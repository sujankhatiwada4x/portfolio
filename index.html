Try it: how a model reads a sentence

Language models don’t read whole words. They cut text into small pieces called tokens. Type a sentence or pick a preset and see how many pieces it takes. The tokenizer below has a small English-leaning vocabulary, so Nepali and Nepal Bhasa fall apart into single characters. This is the problem I faced with a low-resource language.

Dashed pieces are not in the vocabulary, so they are split into single characters. This is a simplified demo, not the real NLLB-200 tokenizer, which is trained on far more text. Languages with less training text still tend to need more pieces per word.
